GLM-OCR (zai-org/GLM-OCR, https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-OCR; code: https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-OCR)

The model is released under the MIT License (see its model card). The vocabulary in assets/glm_ocr/vocab.txt is the vocabulary of its
tokenizer. The model files the app downloads on request (vision.onnx, text.onnx, embeddings.bin; https://huggingface.co/nfa04/glm-ocr-onnx)
are the original model converted to ONNX and quantised to fewer bits after the fact. They were NOT retrained or fine-tuned: nothing
was changed but the number of bits the weights are stored in. For what the model is, how it was trained and how to cite it, see the
original's model card. The original's code repository is under the Apache License 2.0 (none of its code is in the app).
