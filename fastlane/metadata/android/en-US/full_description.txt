Notation is a note-taking and PDF-annotation app for people who write by hand, study, and want their notes to stay theirs.

<b>Write the way you think</b>
• Pen, highlighter and shape tools with pressure, rulers and guides
• Text boxes, tables, checklists and formulas in the same note
• Handwriting turns into searchable text, on the device
• Annotate PDFs; the ink is exported with an invisible text layer

<b>Study</b>
• Flashcards with spaced repetition, quizzes and an Ask mode that answers from your notes
• A maths checker for handwritten working: sums, matrices, inequalities, logic and statistics
• Export flashcards to Anki, notes to Markdown, PDF or LaTeX

<b>Organise</b>
• Folders, tags, search, a calendar with tasks, and reminders
• A graph of the facts your notes state

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• AI features run on the device by default
• Optional live collaboration and sync (Nextcloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, S3, CalDAV), end-to-end encrypted
• Lock notes with a passcode or biometrics

Free software under the AGPL-3.0.
